Common questions

Quick answers

How do I add an audiobook?

Import M4B, MP3, and other supported audio files using the Files picker, AirDrop, a share link, Wi-Fi upload, or a connected Audiobookshelf or Jellyfin server.

Where are my books and listening data stored?

Your audiobook files, progress, bookmarks, folders, and listening statistics are stored locally on your device. The developer cannot see your library or listening history.

How does Skip Silence work?

Skip Silence detects quiet gaps during playback and moves past them based on the sensitivity and timing options you choose. Fine-tune it from the player to match each book.

How do I restore my Skip Silence purchase?

Open the Skip Silence upgrade screen and choose Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Can I turn off anonymous usage statistics?

Yes. Open Settings → Privacy and turn off “Share Anonymous Usage Stats.” The app will stop sending anonymous feature-use events.
